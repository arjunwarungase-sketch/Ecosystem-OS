About Ecosystem OS

Why are we building this?

Ecosystem OS is designed to create a small, self-monitoring ecosystem that can observe its environment and respond to changing conditions with minimal human intervention.

The project combines environmental sensors, embedded computing, automation and artificial intelligence to demonstrate how technology can help maintain a living environment.
